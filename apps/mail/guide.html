Stalwart hosts your email, which you can read on the web with Bulwark, or in any mail app that speaks IMAP.

Mail apps can't use the usual sign-in, so give each its own app password: sign in at $SUBDOMAIN.■/account and create one there. Your username is your email address.

Web
Visit $SUBDOMAIN.■ and sign in.
iOS
In Settings → Apps → Mail → Mail Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Mail Account, enter your address and app password, choose IMAP, and use $URL as both the incoming and outgoing mail server.
Android
Install Thunderbird (or F-Droid), add your address and app password, and it finds the rest itself.
Desktop
In Thunderbird, or most other mail apps, add your address and app password, and it finds the rest itself.

Otherwise, the server is $URL, with IMAP on port 993 and SMTP on port 465, both over SSL/TLS.
